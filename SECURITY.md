# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `hello@unique-utilities.com`. Do not include mailbox passwords, private keys, complete mail archives, or other unnecessary personal data. Include the affected version, impact, and reproducible steps where possible.

The publisher should acknowledge reports promptly, maintain a coordinated vulnerability-disclosure process, publish supported-version periods, and establish the incident and actively exploited vulnerability reporting workflow required by applicable law before commercial distribution in the European Union.

Account secrets use Electron `safeStorage` where operating-system encryption is available. Local mail data and settings are not an end-to-end encrypted vault and remain subject to the security of the Windows account and device. The optional four-character app lock is an access deterrent, not a substitute for Windows sign-in, BitLocker, or a strong device password.
